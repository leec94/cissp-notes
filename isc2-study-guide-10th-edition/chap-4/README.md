# Chapter 4: Laws, Regulations, and Compliance
## Notes
- Privacy Act of 1974: limits the ways government agencies may use info that private citizens disclosure 
- Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA): financial regulation that limited types of info that could be exchanged, and requires providing privacy policies to customers. requires financial institutions protect customer records 
- Electronic Communications Privacy Act (ECPA): implements safeguards against electronic eavesdropping
